# FinLit Bilingüe

**FinLit Bilingüe** is a free, self-contained web app that teaches core personal finance concepts in English and Spanish, side by side — built for adult learners who are more comfortable in Spanish, or still building confidence in English, and want financial literacy without jargon, sales pitches, or a paywall.

## Why this exists

Most financial education online assumes fluent English and prior comfort with money vocabulary. That leaves out a large group of adults who are perfectly capable of managing their finances well but are learning the language of finance and English at the same time. FinLit Bilingüe closes that gap: every concept, every question, and every explanation appears in both languages together — never behind a toggle — so learners can move between them naturally, the way bilingual households actually think and speak.

This is a pure education tool. It does not recommend products, banks, or investments, and it never will — the goal is understanding, not conversion.

## What's inside

**Learn** — A visual introduction to what finance is (personal, corporate, and public finance) and the markets and institutions that move money through the economy: money markets, capital markets, equity and debt markets, primary and secondary markets, banks, insurers, pension funds, mutual funds, and the regulators who oversee them (the SEC, the Federal Reserve, FINRA, and international banking standards like the Basel Accords). From there, six core pillars are explained in plain language: budgeting, saving, credit, investing, risk management, and raising capital.

**Play** — A hike-themed quiz built around six trails, one per pillar. Each trail asks four questions with realistic, sometimes deliberately confusing multiple-choice answers (the wrong options are often real definitions of *other* concepts, so learners have to actually distinguish ideas rather than guess). Every correct answer reveals a piece of a real photograph from Yosemite National Park; finishing a trail reveals the full photo along with a small reward icon and a trail completion badge.

**Audio** — Every English passage has a "listen" button powered by the browser's built-in text-to-speech, so learners can hear pronunciation while reading along — helpful for building vocabulary in a second language.

## How it's built

The entire site is a single HTML file — no server, no database, no account required. It runs entirely in the browser: styling, interactivity, quiz logic, and even the photos (embedded directly as compressed image data) all live in one file. That makes it trivial to host anywhere, share as a link, or save and open offline.

## Who it's for

Adult learners — parents, workers, newcomers to the U.S. financial system — who want to build real financial confidence in whichever language feels more natural to them at any given moment, without being sold anything along the way.

## A note on accuracy

Definitions are written in plain, simplified language for accessibility, but they are checked against standard finance coursework and terminology. This is a teaching tool, not professional financial, legal, or investment advice — for decisions about your own money, consult a qualified, licensed professional.
